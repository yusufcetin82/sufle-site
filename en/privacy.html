Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Sufle collects no personal data. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer receives no information about how you use it.

Your scripts

The scripts you write or import, and your settings, are stored only on your device. They are not sent to any server. When you delete the app, this data is deleted too. The backup feature saves your scripts as a file in a place you choose; where that file is kept is up to you.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you turn on "Voice follow", the app uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition service to match the words you read against the script. Audio is not recorded or stored. If your device supports on-device recognition for the language you are reading, the audio never leaves the device; if it does not, the audio is sent to Apple's servers for speech recognition, which is subject to Apple's privacy policy. While the feature is off, the microphone is never used.

Bluetooth and local network

If you turn on "Remote control", the app uses Bluetooth and the local network so that your own nearby devices (an Apple Watch, another iPhone or iPad) can control the prompter. This connection is only between your devices, is encrypted and does not go over the internet. What is exchanged is commands such as play and pause, plus the title and timing of the open script.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking libraries and shares your data with no one.

Children

The app collects no personal data from any user, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page and the date above is updated.
