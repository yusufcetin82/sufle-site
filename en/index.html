Sufle

A teleprompter for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Sufle lets you read your script while looking straight at the camera, whether you are recording a video or giving a talk. You control the scroll with a Bluetooth remote, an Apple Watch, another iPhone, or simply your voice.

Highlights

  • Smooth scrolling, target duration and rehearsal
  • Voice follow: the text moves as you read
  • Mirror mode, reading line and focus for beam-splitter teleprompters
  • Bluetooth remote, Apple Watch and remote control between devices
  • Import Word, PDF, Markdown and plain text files
