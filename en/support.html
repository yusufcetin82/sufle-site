Support

If something is not working or you have a suggestion, write to us:

The remote's buttons go the wrong way

Open a script and follow Settings → Remote → "Calibrate remote". The app asks you to press the up and down buttons and works out the direction itself. Repeat this step whenever you change how the iPad sits in the teleprompter.

The text looks reversed or sideways on the glass

Under Settings → Screen, switch mirror mode on or off and turn the text with "Text orientation". The screen does not rotate by itself while the iPad lies flat on the teleprompter tray.

Apple Watch does not connect

A script must be open on the device showing the prompter, with the "Remote control" switch on. The watch first tries to connect directly over Bluetooth; if that fails it connects through the paired iPhone. Make sure you allowed the Bluetooth and local network permissions asked for on first use.

Voice follow does not work

In the Settings app, check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed for Sufle. Sufle works out the language of your script by itself; if on-device recognition is not available for that language, an internet connection is needed.

Marks in the script

A line starting with # Heading becomes a section. Words between **two asterisks** are emphasised. / and // with a space on each side are breath marks. The scroll stops wherever you write [PAUSE]. The "Writing guide" in the app has examples.
